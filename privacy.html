Spotter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Spotter is a workout and nutrition tracker published on Google Play by Lingaas Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: lingaas.studio@gmail.com.

Using Spotter as a guest

If you choose "Open app as Guest", your workouts, routines, streaks and food logs are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Data we collect when you sign in

Signing in with Google creates a Spotter account through Google Firebase Authentication. We then store:

  • Account details: your name, email address, profile picture and a user ID, as provided by your Google account.
  • Fitness data you enter: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, routines, streaks and profile settings.
  • Nutrition data you enter: food logs, calories and macros.
  • Leaderboard and social data: your display name, streak and workout totals, which other signed-in Spotter users can see.

We use this data only to run the app: to sync your data across devices, show your progress and run the leaderboard and social features. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers. Spotter contains no ads.

AI Food & Macro Scanner

When you use the scanner, the photo or text you submit is sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic so it can estimate nutrition. It is processed only to return the estimate. Spotter does not keep the photo; only the resulting food log entry is saved if you choose to save it.

Service providers

Spotter runs on Google services, which process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, AI Logic). Data is stored in Google's europe-west2 (London) region.
  • Google Play (app distribution and app integrity checks).

See Firebase privacy and security and the Google Privacy Policy.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Database security rules ensure that only you can read or change your own data. Leaderboard entries can be read by other signed-in users but only changed by you.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data until you delete it. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your account, fitness and nutrition data and your leaderboard entry within 30 days.

Your rights

If you live in the EEA, the UK or Norway, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet). Our legal basis for processing is to provide the service you ask for (contract). Email us to exercise these rights.

Children

Spotter is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
