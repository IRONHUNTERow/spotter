Delete your Spotter account

Spotter by Lingaas Studio. You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Spotter and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email lingaas.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Spotter account", from the Google account email you signed in with. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Spotter account (name, email, profile picture, user ID)
  • Workouts, exercises, routines and streaks
  • Food and nutrition logs
  • Your leaderboard entry, friend requests and friendships
  • Your cloud backup

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires it. Guest data that is stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Delete some data, but keep your account

You can delete individual workouts, routines and food logs in the app. To have other specific data removed, such as your leaderboard entry, email us at the address above and say what you want deleted.
